Amazon Store Outpost Privacy Policy

Amazon Store Outpost is a private internal-use tool used to access, analyze, report on, and operate authorized Amazon selling and advertising accounts on behalf of their owners.

Effective Date: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when this application is connected to Amazon services, including Login with Amazon, the Amazon Selling Partner API (SP-API), and the Amazon Ads API.

1. Information We Collect

When an Amazon selling account and/or Amazon Ads account is authorized for use with this application, we may access and process:

  • Amazon seller and Ads account and profile identifiers (seller ID, marketplace ID, advertising profile ID)
  • Orders, shipments, returns, refunds, and settlement reports
  • Sales and traffic reports, inventory, catalog listings, and pricing data
  • Brand Analytics, coupons, promotions, and competitive pricing reports
  • Advertising campaigns, ad groups, keywords, targeting, and performance reports
  • OAuth refresh tokens used to call the SP-API and Ads API on the seller's behalf
  • Basic contact information you provide to us, if any

We do not collect or store your Amazon password through this application. Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from Amazon orders (such as buyer names, addresses, or phone numbers) is not retained beyond what is strictly required to operate authorized workflows, and is handled in accordance with Amazon's Data Protection Policy and Acceptable Use Policy.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information only to:

  • connect to authorized Amazon selling and advertising accounts,
  • retrieve orders, settlements, inventory, advertising data, and other reports,
  • monitor store and campaign performance,
  • support reporting and internal analysis,
  • improve store operations, campaign management, and optimization workflows,
  • maintain, debug, and secure the application.

We do not use Amazon Selling Partner or Amazon Ads data for purposes unrelated to the operation, reporting, management, or optimization of the authorized accounts.

3. Who Can Use This Tool

This application is intended only for private internal use by Avatech Corp. in connection with Amazon selling and advertising accounts that Avatech Corp. is authorized to operate.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell Amazon Selling Partner data, Amazon Ads data, or personal information.

We may share information only when necessary:

  • with hosting, cloud, analytics, or technical service providers who support operation of the tool,
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request,
  • to protect the security, integrity, or lawful operation of the application.

5. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the tool, maintain reporting history, support optimization workflows, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

When data is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymize it where reasonably practical.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Third-Party Services

This application interacts with third-party services provided by Amazon, including Login with Amazon, the Amazon Selling Partner API, and the Amazon Ads API. Use of those services is also subject to Amazon's own terms, notices, and policies, including the Amazon Services API Developer Agreement, the Amazon Acceptable Use Policy, and the Amazon Data Protection Policy.

8. Your Choices

If you want to stop allowing this application to access your Amazon selling or advertising account, you may revoke access at any time through Seller Central (Apps & Services → Manage Your Apps), through the relevant Amazon Ads account settings, or by contacting us at the email below.
